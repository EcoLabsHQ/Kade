4. The model
THESIS  Pre-approve on payment, review in the background. Residents get value on day one; the programme keeps a machine-verified queue and intervenes only on exception.
PRE-APPROVAL FEE  Production: XCG 500 + XCG 100 per household member, settled in USDC (household of 3 → 447 USDC). Paid at signup, pre-approves instantly. Paid on Ethereum mainnet.
RESIDENCY SERVICE FEE  Production: 2,000 USDC, payable only once four checks are green. Covers package compilation and submission, household permits, biometrics scheduling and Kranshi registration. Paid on Ethereum mainnet.
QUALIFYING TIERS  3-year $280k · 5-year $425k · Indefinite $850k. Only assets inside a qualified vault count — summed across Ethereum, Arbitrum and Base.
GOS FEES  Fund vault 0.5%; cash out 1%. Same fee on every chain; bridging cost is absorbed by Kade at demo scale and itemised in production.
CHAINS  Ethereum mainnet is the main network: identity Safe, all attestations, both programme fees, the GOS settlement leg. Arbitrum and Base carry qualified vaults, deposits, yield positions and can be the source or destination of card top-ups and fiat legs. Safe smart accounts (ERC-4337) at the same address on all three; USDC for all value; gas sponsored by a Kade paymaster on each chain.
DATA  Supabase (Postgres + Auth + Realtime + RLS) owns workflow; the chains own money and attestations.

Identity verification with Self
Identity is proven, not uploaded. Kade uses Self (self.xyz) so the programme never holds a passport copy for the checks it can prove instead.
The resident scans their passport's NFC chip in the Self app; Self generates a zero-knowledge proof from the passport's signed data.
Kade receives proofs, not documents: nationality disclosed, over-18 proven, not-on-sanctions-list proven, plus a nullifier that guarantees one verification per passport without revealing which passport. Passport number, photograph and date of birth are never transmitted to us.
The proof is verified onchain and written to the resident's Safe as kade.kyc.v1, then reused by the card issuer, the banking partner and the Snapshot voting strategy — verify once, reuse everywhere.
Verification happens inside the platform after pre-approval (it is readiness check 1 of 4 on the Start Residency screen), not during pre-registration.

8. Architecture
FRONTEND  Next.js 14 + wagmi/viem, chains configured for mainnet (1), Arbitrum One (42161) and Base (8453). 240px sidebar + 1160px content column; Plus Jakarta Sans; #0063B5 / #0A7CFF / #101828 / #667085 / #FAFAFA.
ACCOUNTS  Safe{Core} + 4337; passkey via Privy or the Safe WebAuthn signer; identical Safe address on all three chains (same owners, same salt, same factory). Pimlico paymaster per chain, restricted to Kade contracts, EAS, the Self verifier contract and USDC → treasury, with a per-account daily cap — identity verification calls are sponsored.
ATTESTATIONS  EAS on Ethereum mainnet: kade.kyc.v1 (nationality · over18 · notSanctioned · selfNullifier · expiresAt), kade.fee.v1, kade.application.v1, kade.review.v1, kade.permit.v1. Schema UIDs published in the README so anyone can verify a permit.
IDENTITY  Self verifies a zero-knowledge passport proof onchain; the verifier contract is on the paymaster allowlist and on the KadeLock allowlist so a locked vault can still call it. Self supports Celo, Ethereum and several L2s, so proof verification can happen on whichever chain is cheapest and the resulting kade.kyc.v1 attestation is mirrored to the resident's primary chain. Kade stores the attestation UID and the disclosed fields only — never the raw proof payload.
QUALIFIED VAULT  Safe Guard/Module KadeLock, one deployment per chain, same bytecode: blocks outbound value before unlockAt except allowlisted addresses (treasury fee address, approved instruments on that chain, GOS settlement address, the CCTP TokenMessenger, the Self verifier contract — verification calls are sponsored and carry no value); compliance co-signer 2-of-2 on withdrawals; cure event when the cross-chain sum of value < tier, evaluated by the indexer and written as a container_balances row.
ACCOUNTING RULE  Qualified total = Σ over chains of (vault assets + instrument allocations). An allocation debits vault USDC and credits an instrument row on the same chain, so the qualified total is unchanged and Total = Qualified + Not qualified always holds — per chain and in aggregate. A bridge in flight counts on the source chain until the CCTP mint lands.
FEES  Each programme fee is a USDC transfer to the Treasury Safe on mainnet batched with its attestations via MultiSend — one signature.
FIAT RAILS & CARD  GOS API for on/off-ramp and card issuing; GOS settles on Ethereum mainnet. A Kade relayer (Edge Function + hot wallet, daily cap) bridges native USDC with Circle CCTP v2 between mainnet and Arbitrum / Base whenever the user's chosen chain differs from the settlement chain. Card settles XCG at the CBCS peg (1.79) against the chosen vault balance.
VOTING  Snapshot space onchainisland.eth; strategy checks kade.kyc.v1 on mainnet; gasless EIP-712.
INDEXER  One worker polls balances and vault events on all three chains (Alchemy or a Helius-equivalent per chain), writes container_balances and wealth_snapshots with a chain column, and emits the cure event. Never block a render on a chain read.

14. Development tips
Money and attestations onchain, workflow in Supabase. Never block a render on a chain read.
Drive every amount from fee_schedule and every address from chains; flipping demo → production, or adding a fourth chain, must be a config change with no code edit.
Cap the paymaster and the relayer hot wallet per account per day and alert on anomalies — this is mainnet plus two L2s.
Everything starts empty: proof of wealth $0, no qualified assets, no card. Gate the Continue buttons instead of pre-filling data.
Keep the accounting invariant in a test: Total Holdings = Qualified + Not qualified, before and after every allocation, transfer and bridge, per chain and in aggregate.
Seed six applications so the Admin lanes look alive; approving the demo applicant must unlock the resident account in real time.
Documents: hash + pin, attest the root, show the CID. Uploads live in Residency, not pre-registration.
Self gives us proofs, not PII: never store the raw proof payload, only the attestation UID and the disclosed fields we are legally required to keep.
Treat the nullifier as the primary identity key for duplicate detection, not name + date of birth.
Keep a manual-review fallback for passports Self does not support (non-NFC or unsupported issuing countries) — a document-upload path gated to admin review.
Commit hygiene is judged: real names, small commits, conventional messages, PRs for anything larger than a fix. No bulk import of pre-existing code without a disclosed provenance.
UI conventions: stat cards #FAFAFA + #EAEDF1; clickable cards hover #0A7CFF; locked actions #98A2B3 + cursor: not-allowed; green #1B7F5A, amber #B7791F, red #B42318; chain chips ETH / ARB / BASE in #667085 outline; equal-height cards via align-items: stretch plus a flex spacer above the CTA.
